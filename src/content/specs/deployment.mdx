## Deployment & Edge Architecture

Kaarya operates as a dual-runtime, single-binary industrial operations ledger. It embeds a complete Go backend, an industrial SQLite database, and a high-performance Nuxt 4 frontend directly into a desktop application without external database daemons or cloud requirements.
