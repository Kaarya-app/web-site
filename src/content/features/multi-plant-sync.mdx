## Alpha R&D Status
Currently in early architectural prototyping with design partners in Pune and Bengaluru. Designed with a store-and-forward buffer architecture to guarantee zero data loss even during multi-day factory WAN outages.

### Sync Modes
| Mode | Use Case | Data Freshness |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Real-time (WAN)** | Connected plants, reliable fiber | < 5 sec |
| **Scheduled (Daily)** | Intermittent connectivity, cost-sensitive | 24 hr batches |
| **Manual (Air-gap export)** | Defence/ITAR plants, zero outbound | On-demand |

### What Federates
- **OEE & TEEP** — Rollup by plant, cell, machine type
- **Scrap & Quality** — Pareto across sites, supplier comparison
- **Energy** — kWh/part benchmarking, demand charge optimization
- **Compliance Status** — Audit readiness dashboard per plant
- **Asset Health** — MTTR/MTBF, predictive maintenance alerts

### Security
- gRPC over mTLS with mutual authentication
- End-to-end encryption (keys never leave edge nodes)
- 500MB local buffer — survives extended outages
- Air-gap export: Signed JSON bundle → USB → Import at HQ
