## Auditor-Ready Shift Reports & Data Portability

Exporting manufacturing data should not require waiting for background batch queues or dealing with mangled CSV text encodings. Kaarya provides fast, synchronous reporting straight from the shopfloor ledger:

### Formal PDF Shift Dossiers
- **Built in Go (`gofpdf`):** Generates multi-page formatted reports in under 2 seconds without external browser automation or headless Chrome overhead.
- **Foundry Industrial Layout:** Branded headers, executive KPI summary strips, detailed production log tables, and machine/worker breakdown sections.
- **Financial & Scrap Costing:** Automatically includes scrap costs and machine downtime cost-per-hour totals.

### Excel-Optimized Denormalized CSV
- **UTF-8 with Byte Order Mark (BOM):** Opens seamlessly in Microsoft Excel without character encoding corruptions or CSV delimiter prompt issues.
- **Denormalized Metrics:** Pre-calculates Availability, Performance, Quality, and overall OEE across every recorded run for direct pivot table analysis.

### Secure Role-Based Filtering
- **Smart Date Defaults:** Pre-populates the modal with the "Last 7 Days" and performs real-time date range validation.
- **Access Guardrails:** Restricts floor operators to a maximum 30-day window while granting administrators up to 90 days of export history.
