## Mobile Floor Companion
Kaarya Mobile (`apps/mobile`) brings real-time shopfloor telemetry straight to operators and supervisors on the plant floor:

### Operator Mode (Core)
- **Instant Andon Notifications**: Get alerted to machine stoppages, micro-stops, or tool jams within milliseconds
- **Fast Downtime Logging**: Categorize breakdown reasons at the machine side without walking back to an office PC
- **7 Indian Languages**: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali — operators tap in their language
- **Voice Input**: Speak downtime reason with gloved hands; auto-categorized
- **Mandatory Reason Gate**: Cannot tap "Resume" without selecting a downtime reason
- **Shift Handover Log**: Digital, signed, searchable — replaces WhatsApp/whiteboard
- **Part Barcode Scanning**: Scan → Log → Traceability chain complete

### Supervisor Mode (Pro)
- **Dispatch & Escalation**: Assign breakdowns to maintenance, quality, tooling with SLA timers
- **Real-Time Cell View**: All machines in cell on one screen — Red/Amber/Green + OEE %
- **Shift Summary**: Auto-generated shift report (OEE, downtime Pareto, quality, notes) for shift handover
- **Biometric Operator Auth** (Pro): Face/Touch ID for operator accountability

### Air-Gapped Local LAN Discovery
Automatically connects to the on-premise Kaarya server node via UDP broadcast (:9955) — zero internet or cloud connection required. Works in Faraday-cage shops, defence facilities, and remote plants.
