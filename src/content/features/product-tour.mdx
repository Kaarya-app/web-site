## Role-Based Shopfloor Onboarding

Manufacturing floor staff have distinct operational duties and technical backgrounds. Kaarya replaces heavy printed manuals with contextual, non-intrusive interactive guides directly in the UI:

### Modular Tour Tracks
- **Operator Essentials (3 Steps):** Highlights the live Machine Andon grid, one-tap shift & scrap logging, and the downtime call-for-help button.
- **Plant Manager / OEE Walkthrough (4 Steps):** Explains the primary OEE KPI strip, machine efficiency heatmap, one-click PDF/CSV export, and financial scrap costing.
- **Admin Setup Tour (3 Steps):** Guides initial machine and shift configuration, worker roster management, and LAN server node pairing.

### Engineered for the Factory Floor
- **100% Offline & Air-Gapped:** Bundled inside the application; requires zero external CDN requests or tracking scripts.
- **Foundry Industrial Aesthetics:** High-contrast dark backdrop (`#101a35`), engineered accent indicators (Action Cobalt & Safety Green), and crisp IBM Plex Mono step indicators.
- **Persistent & On-Demand:** Remembers completion via local storage and offers immediate relaunch anytime from the sidebar help menu or settings.
