## Performance-Driven Shopfloor Payroll

In precision manufacturing, operator compensation and shopfloor profitability rely directly on attendance and cycle time efficiency. When operators lack visibility into their performance targets, efficiency drops and payroll disputes rise.

Kaarya Pro introduces the **Worker Efficiency & Attendance Payroll Ledger** — a digital shopfloor matrix marrying automated shift attendance tracking with engineered cycle-time efficiency scoring.

### Dual-View Matrix Architecture

| View Mode | Focus | Captured Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily Attendance & Efficiency Heatmap** | Operator $\times$ Date | Day-by-day status (`P`, `A`, `HD`, `L`), efficiency %, actual vs standard cycle time, base wage, incentive bonus, total gross pay |
| **Part / SKU Performance Matrix** | Operator $\times$ Part | Product output, scrap count, SKU-specific cycle time benchmarks, operator skill mapping |

### Dynamic Efficiency Color Thresholds

The Foundry Industrial heatmap highlights operator productivity using industrial performance bands:
- 🟢 **World-Class ($\ge 85\%$):** Meets or exceeds engineered standard cycle times; qualifies for performance incentive bonus.
- 🟡 **Standard ($65\%\text{--}85\%$):** Normal operating pace without cycle speed loss.
- 🔴 **Critical ($<65\%$):** Excessive cycle time deviations, setup delays, or micro-stoppages requiring supervisor investigation.
- ⚪ **Absent / Rest:** Clean neutral indicator for off days or unlogged shifts.

### Automatic Attendance with Supervisor Audit Overrides

1. **Automatic Shift Extraction:** Every production log submitted from floor terminals automatically registers worker attendance and calculates active production hours.
2. **Manual Overrides:** Supervisors can mark approved leaves, half-days, or authorized shift swaps directly from the heatmap cell drilldown with audit timestamps and reason notes.
3. **One-Click Payroll CSV Export:** Formatted with UTF-8 BOM for instant opening in Microsoft Excel and ERP accounting systems.
